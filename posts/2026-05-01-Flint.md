This map was created in order to find areas in Flint, Michigan that would be most likely to have poisonous tap water. These areas were defined as being:
    Within .3 miles of a TRI facility with water pollution over 10,000 tons
    Within .3 miles of a river
    Within .2 miles with water service lines made of lead

Tools used inlude:
    Select by attribute 
    Buffer
    Join
    Intersect
