Displayed here is the density of Airbnb's per square mile across Chicago. This map was created in order to compare the spatial patterns of Airbnb listings within the city of Chicago to the spatial distribution of homicides within the city. The homicide density map is also displayed on this site. Please view them in tandem. As you can see, AirBNB listings are concentrated along the north eastern section of the city, along the water front.
