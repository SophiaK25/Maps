Here is displayed the density of Airbnb's per square mile across Chicago. This map was created in order to compare the spatial patterns of Airbnb listings within the city of Chicago. As you can see, they are concentrated along the north eastern section of the city, along the water front. This map was created in tandem with the homicide density map also displayed on this site. Please view them together.
