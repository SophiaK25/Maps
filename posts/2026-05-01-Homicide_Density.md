Here you can see the density of homicides across the city of Chicago. You can see that homocides appear to occur at the highest density within police districts 11 and 15. This map was created in order to compare the spatial patterns of homicides within the city of Chicago to the spatial distribution of AirBNB listings within the city. The AirBNB listing density map is also displayed on this site. Please view them in tandem.
